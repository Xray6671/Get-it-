About Nevada Business Watch

Every Nevada business carries a paper trail — licenses, registrations, permits, insurance documents, renewals and government filings. We built Nevada Business Watch to keep that trail in order.

Many business owners don’t need another software platform. They need someone to help organize the information, track important dates and identify items that may need attention. Nevada Business Watch provides hands-on administrative monitoring and research support for Nevada businesses, contractors, and nonprofits.

  • Not a law firm.
  • Not an accounting firm.
  • Not an insurance agency.
  • Not a software platform.

We’re hands-on administrative support.

Our Approach

Running a business involves more than serving customers. Business owners, contractors, and nonprofit leaders also have to keep track of licenses, registrations, permits, insurance documents, workers’ compensation information, renewals, government correspondence, and other records.

Nevada Business Watch helps organize that information and monitor the items you ask us to track.

We review available information, organize business records, track important dates and documents, and identify items that may need attention.

You run your business. We help you keep track of the paperwork behind it.

Hands-On Service — Not Just Another Software Platform

Nevada Business Watch is designed for business owners and nonprofit leaders who want a real person helping them, not another complicated software system they have to learn and manage themselves.

We use technology to organize information and communicate with clients, but the service is hands-on and personal.

You can work directly with us to organize your business information, submit documents, request research, and keep track of items that may need attention.
