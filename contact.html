01 — Questions

Common questions

Do you file my renewals?

No. We flag the date and you file and pay. This keeps you in control of every filing.

Are government fees included?

No. City, state, contractor board, and insurance fees go directly to the agency.

What do I need to send you?

Photos or PDFs of the licenses, permits, insurance, and business papers you already have. Messy is fine. Contractors can start with just a license number, and we look up what is public.

What is a public-record check?

We look at one official website for one record, such as a license lookup, and write the result into your file that day. It is a snapshot, not a legal determination. We only access public records — we do not conduct surveillance, interviews, or private investigations, and our research is not a “consumer report” under federal law, so it should not be used to screen an employee, tenant, or credit applicant.

I have a shop and a contractor license. What do I choose?

Each business or license needs its own file. Contact us and we will quote both together.

I am not licensed yet. Can you help?

To get your first contractor license you need a licensing school or a license filer. Come back once you are licensed, and we will watch the dates.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. Choose English or Español when you sign up, and your reports and notices come in that language.

What if I cancel?

Monitoring stops. You keep copies of everything we organized.

Do you work with nonprofits?

Yes. Nonprofit Watch tracks your nonprofit’s state status, charitable solicitation registration, and IRS Form 990 deadline the same way we track business and contractor records.

Do you give legal advice?

No. We provide administrative organization and monitoring only. We do not give legal, tax, or insurance advice, or represent you before any agency.
