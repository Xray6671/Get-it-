01 — Sound Familiar?

Before the deadline becomes the problem.

A license expiration isn’t the only thing that matters. Sometimes it’s:

  • “Where is that certificate?”
  • “Did we renew that license?”
  • “When does our policy expire?”
  • “Which jurisdiction issued this?”
  • “Did we send the updated document?”
  • “Who has the current copy?”

That’s the administrative work we help organize.

02 — The Difference

You don’t need another dashboard.
You need someone watching the file.

Most small businesses don’t need another login, another spreadsheet, or another piece of software to manage. They need the paperwork organized.

  • We maintain the file.
  • We monitor the dates.
  • We identify items that need attention.
  • You decide what action to take.

03 — Process

How it works

Start small. Your file grows from there.

1

Start with a check

Request the Free Business Check below, or order a full Business Check or Contractor Check. We review your records against public sources.

2

We build your file

You provide your business information and available documents. We organize your licenses, policies, and dates into one file and a status report that shows what is current, what is missing, and what needs attention.

3

We watch it every month

With a Watch plan, we track your dates and documents and flag anything approaching before it becomes a problem. You file and pay the agency.

4

We tell you

You receive a simple update when something needs attention.

You take action. Renew it. Update it. Contact your agent. Submit the document.

We keep the file moving.
