Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with Nevada Business Watch.

Effective Date: September 25, 2026

Overview

Nevada Business Watch LLC (“Nevada Business Watch,” “we,” “us”) provides administrative record-organization and compliance-monitoring services to Nevada business owners and contractors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and how you can reach us with questions. It applies to this website and to the information you give us when you request a Business Check, sign up for a Watch plan, or contact us.

Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to give us, primarily through the contact form on this site. That form may ask for:

  • Your name
  • Business name
  • City
  • Whether you are a business owner, contractor, both, or other
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Any additional details you choose to share in the message field

When you submit the contact form, your answers are delivered to our email inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-delivery service that processes them only to deliver them to us. If that service is unavailable, the site instead opens your own email app so you can send the request yourself.

If you become a client, we also collect the business records you provide so we can build and monitor your Business File — for example, license numbers, insurance certificates, workers’ compensation policy information, permits, and related renewal dates. We collect this directly from you or, where noted in our services, by reviewing selected public records.

For Workplace Safety & Heat Compliance services, we may also collect information about your work sites, tasks, safety practices, and past workplace incidents, plus the names and signatures of employees who attend training. We ask clients not to share employee medical details beyond what an incident record requires.

We do not process payments on this website and do not collect or store credit card numbers, bank account numbers, or other payment credentials through this site. Any billing for our services is handled separately and is not part of this website’s data collection.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your Free Business Check request or other inquiry
  • To prepare a quote and, if you sign up, to build and maintain your Business File
  • To monitor selected dates and documents and to notify you when something needs attention
  • To communicate with you in English or Español, based on your preference
  • To keep basic business records, such as invoices and correspondence, for our own recordkeeping

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies and Website Analytics

This website uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused analytics service that does not use cookies and does not collect or store any personal information about visitors. It does not track you across other websites or devices, and the traffic data it reports to us is aggregated and anonymous — we use it only to understand general visit trends and which pages and sample-report links people use, so we can improve the site. We do not use advertising cookies or ad-tracking scripts of any kind. The site does load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) so that pages display correctly; as with any external resource, the font provider may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type as part of that request. Our web hosting provider may also keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address, date, and page requested) for security and maintenance purposes. We do not otherwise track your browsing activity on this site.

How We Protect Information

We take reasonable, industry-standard precautions to protect the information you share with us, including limiting access to client records to the people who need it to do their work. No method of storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep client business records for as long as needed to provide our services and for a reasonable period afterward for our own business and recordkeeping purposes. If you contact us but do not become a client, we keep your inquiry only as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and maintain basic business records.

Your Choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we have on file for you, to correct it, or to delete information we are not otherwise required to keep. You can also ask us to stop contacting you. To make any of these requests, use the contact information below.

Children’s Privacy

This website and our services are directed to business owners and contractors and are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services or this website change. The effective date above shows when this version took effect.
