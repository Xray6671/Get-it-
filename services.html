01 — Services

Six ways we help

Pick the one that fits. Most businesses start with Business Watch or Contractor Watch.

We watch and tell you — we don’t file for you. Your licenses, insurance, and renewals stay in your name and under your control. 04 — Status

A living file — not a filing cabinet.

Your Business File is continuously updated as documents and information change.

Current

Records appear current based on the information reviewed.

Attention

Something is approaching expiration, missing, inconsistent, or needs follow-up.

Expired / Overdue

A date has passed or a required record needs immediate attention.

Information Needed

We need a document, clarification, or updated information from you.

This is better than saying “Compliant / Non-Compliant.” That distinction matters because your business is ultimately responsible for its legal and regulatory compliance. Your service is administrative monitoring and record organization, not legal certification.
